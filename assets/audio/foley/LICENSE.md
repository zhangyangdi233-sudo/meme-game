# Carpet footsteps

“steps_carpet.wav” by **Mihacappy**, published March 15, 2026.

Source: https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/848210/

License: **Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal**
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The public Ogg preview is retained alongside four 0.48-second mono excerpts.
Edits: segmentation, normalization and short edge fades. The original recording
is not used for model training. Exact segment offsets and download URL are in
`carpet_steps_source.json`; `tools/prepare_carpet_foley.py` reproduces the cuts.
